Pharmacy Voice AI Device

Hardware + software, built solo

Pharmacy cleanroom staff need quick answers — dosing guidelines, BUD tables, procedures — but their hands and eyes are occupied, and phones aren't allowed. I built a pocket-sized device that answers spoken questions instantly, running entirely on hardware I control.

Press and hold a button, ask a question, get a spoken answer back within seconds. Under the hood: Whisper for speech-to-text, Claude for reasoning, a local RAG pipeline over the pharmacy's own reference documents, and a voice-locked PIN system for security. It's in active use at Stoneview's compounding cleanroom today.

Raspberry Pi · Whisper · Claude · Ollama + Qdrant · TypeScript/Node.js
